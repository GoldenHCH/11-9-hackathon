Why it works

Quest slots are an auction, like search ads

Sponsors bid what a booth visit is worth to them. Elasticsearch scores how well each sponsor fits each attendee’s goals and notes. Rank is bid × fit, and the winner pays just enough to beat the next sponsor. A bigger budget can’t buy quests for people who don’t care, so attendees trust the quests and keep taking them.
